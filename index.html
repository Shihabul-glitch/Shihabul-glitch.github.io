Thesis project

Low-Cost Dual-Core Flight Controller for Quadcopter

Designed and developed a custom flight controller from scratch for quadcopter applications, focusing on cost-effectiveness and reliability. The system was tested under partial propeller disturbance conditions to evaluate performance and robustness.

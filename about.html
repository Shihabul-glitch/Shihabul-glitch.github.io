About

About Me

PCB designer and embedded systems engineer, with a passion for drones, planes and anything that flies.

My passion & expertise

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate working as a freelance PCB layout engineer. My focus is PCB design, from high-speed Ethernet and isolated high-voltage power to compact, low-power boards, and the embedded systems that run on them. My favourite place to apply it is anything that flies: I design the flight controllers for my own drones and planes.

My thesis focused on creating a low-cost dual-core flight controller for quadcopters and testing its performance under partial propeller disturbance conditions. I'm passionate about making aerial robotics accessible and affordable while maintaining high performance and reliability.

Beyond technical work, I've helped re-establish and lead the Robotics Club at KUET, mentoring over 100 students in hardware and robotics fundamentals. I believe in sharing knowledge and inspiring the next generation of engineers to explore electronics, robotics and UAV technology.

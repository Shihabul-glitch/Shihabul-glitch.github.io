PCB Studio: Blender extension for realistic PCB renders

Altium's 3D view is good for checking a design, but not for presenting it. PCB Studio turns an Altium OBJ + MTL export into a realistic product render or turntable animation in a few clicks, for engineers who don't know Blender. The Vector Drive render on the left was made with it.

  • Imports OBJ and auto-detects the linked MTL materials
  • PCB material presets, studio lighting rigs and HDRI modes
  • Camera presets, automatic framing and turntable animation
  • Denoised Cycles stills with automatic GPU/CPU selection
  • Tested on Blender 4.5 LTS and 5.2 LTS

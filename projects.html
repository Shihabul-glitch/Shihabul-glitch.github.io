UAV & Flight Control Systems

Thesis project

Low-Cost Dual-Core Flight Controller for Quadcopter

Designed and developed a custom flight controller from scratch for quadcopter applications. Features dual-core processing, sensor fusion, PID stabilization, and real-time telemetry. The system was tested under partial propeller disturbance conditions to evaluate performance, reliability, and fault tolerance in challenging flight scenarios.

Research impact Demonstrates cost-effective flight control and establishes performance benchmarks for UAV systems under adverse conditions.

ESP32 Mini Drone with PCB Frame

Designed the schematic and PCB in KiCad; the PCB doubles as the airframe, with the arms and motor mounts built into the board. Inspired by Espressif's ESP-Drone. Manufactured by JLCPCB in framed and frameless versions, and it flies.

Evolution Chose a BMI088 IMU over the MPU6050 after real-world tests. An earlier EasyEDA prototype with an MPU6050, milled in the KUET fabrication lab, also flew.

Mini Plane: ESP8266 Flight Controller PCBs

Designed two ESP8266 flight controller PCBs in KiCad for a smartphone-controlled mini plane flown over Wi-Fi: a no-IMU version with two DC motor drivers for the self-stable airframe, and a BMI088 IMU version with four motor outputs, sponsored by JLCPCB.

Control Implemented a PID roll stabilizer, first tested with an MPU6050.

PCB Studio — Blender Extension for PCB Renders

A Blender extension that turns Altium PCB exports (OBJ + MTL) into professional product renders and turntable animations, built for engineers who don't know Blender. One-click scene setup, PCB material presets, studio lighting, camera presets and Cycles/EEVEE rendering.

Tested on Blender 4.5 LTS and 5.2 LTS, with an automated test suite.

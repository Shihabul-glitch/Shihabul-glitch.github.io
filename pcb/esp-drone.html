PCB Design / ESP32 Mini Drone

Own design · KiCad · JLCPCB

ESP32 Mini Drone with PCB Frame

A mini drone whose PCB is also the airframe: the arms and motor mounts are part of the board. I designed the schematic and PCB in KiCad, inspired by Espressif's ESP-Drone. It flies.

ToolKiCad
My roleSchematic and PCB layout
IMUBMI088
ManufacturingJLCPCB, framed and frameless versions
StatusFlight-tested

Design highlights

  • The PCB is the airframe. Arms and motor mounts are part of the board, so no separate frame is needed.
  • BMI088 over MPU6050. I compared both IMUs in real-world tests and chose the BMI088 for its better performance.
  • Antenna keep-out zone kept clear of copper, as visible in both layout views.
  • From prototype to production. An earlier EasyEDA prototype with an MPU6050 was milled in the KUET fabrication lab (post) and also flew (video).

Mini Plane Flight Controller PCBs

Two ESP8266 flight controller boards for a smartphone-controlled mini plane, flown over Wi-Fi from a phone. I designed the schematic and PCB of both in KiCad.

ToolKiCad
MCUESP8266
ControlWi-Fi from a smartphone
VersionsNo-IMU (2 motors) and BMI088 IMU (4 motor outputs)
SponsorJLCPCB sponsored the IMU version

Design highlights

  • No-IMU version. Two unidirectional DC motor drivers. It needs no IMU or PID loop because the mini plane's airframe is self-stable: thrown by hand, it glides nicely.
  • BMI088 IMU version. Adds a BMI088 IMU for stabilization and four motor outputs.
  • PID roll stabilizer. First tested with an MPU6050 (flight demo).

The two boards

1 · BMI088 IMU version

ESP8266 with a BMI088 IMU and four motor outputs. PCB sponsored by JLCPCB.

2 · No-IMU version

ESP8266 with two unidirectional DC motor drivers, for the self-stable mini plane airframe.
